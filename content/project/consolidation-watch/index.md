## Overview

You can't assess the resilience of what you can't observe.

Consolidation Watch develops the measurements, methods, and frameworks needed to make Internet infrastructure consolidation visible — to distinguish concentration that reflects ecosystem constraints from concentration that reflects choices — so that its consequences can be understood and addressed.
