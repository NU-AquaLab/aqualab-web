## Motivation and Approach

TrailBlaze Chicago was an app for Android-owning cyclists in the Chicago area —
one of the most bike-friendly big cities in America, and one that had worked
hard to become so. But a city can only keep improving its network if it knows
where new paths are needed, and that information sits with the people already
riding.

TrailBlaze recorded and anonymously reported a rider's tracks, letting them vote
for new paths simply by carrying their phone while doing what they already
enjoyed. It also gave the rider something back: a dashboard with distance,
speed, and a map, plus the ability to review previously reported routes.

The app came **2nd in the Apps 4 Metro Chicago Grand Challenge**, and **4th in
the Transportation Round** — first among the not-for-profit entries.

It was distributed through the Android Market and is no longer available.
