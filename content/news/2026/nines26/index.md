Rashna Kumar and Mia Weaver each presented a paper at the **New Ideas in Networked Systems (NINeS) 2026**, at the New England Pod held at MIT, on February 10, 2026:

- Rashna presented **"Who Holds the Steering Wheel? Opacity and Consolidation in CDN Replica Selection"** (*Rashna Kumar, Fabián E. Bustamante, Marcel Flores*)

- Mia presented **"Monitoring Latency on Submarine Cables: Limitations and Opportunities"** (*Mia Weaver, Darryl Veitch, Paul Barford, Fabián E. Bustamante, Esteban Carisimo*)
